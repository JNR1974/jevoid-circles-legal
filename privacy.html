Jevoid Circles (the “App”) is intended to help people coordinate with trusted groups (“Circles”) using privacy-conscious features. The development implementation includes account registration and sign-in, and Circle creation, administration, membership and expiry controls. Circle invitations, optional location sharing, meetups, journeys and SOS features are planned or not yet launched. This notice must be updated before those features are activated for users.

1. Who controls your data?

Jevoid Circles is the public-facing name of the service. Its data controller is Jordi Nogues, established in Ireland. Contact for privacy matters: LensLabsAI@gmail.com. As an Ireland-based sole trader, the controller will confirm any applicable trader identity and postal-address disclosures before launch.

2. Information we process

  • Accounts and authentication: email address, Firebase account identifier, email-verification state and authentication provider; Google Sign-In may supply basic profile data such as display name and image, subject to the information actually requested.
  • Profile and consent records: chosen display name, optional image, eligibility declarations and accepted versions/timestamps of notices where those flows are enabled.
  • Device and service security: installation identifiers, App Check attestation/debug credentials during development, service diagnostics, abuse-prevention counters and device-registration metadata where enabled. We do not intend to publish debug credentials or access tokens.
  • Circle and future safety data: Circle names, types, membership, roles, ownership and expiry are represented in the current development design. Invitations, meetup details, voluntary location-sharing sessions, journeys and emergency communications will be processed only if those features are later enabled; their precise fields, recipients and retention must be confirmed before activation.
  • Support messages: content you voluntarily submit when contacting us.

4. Sharing with others

Firebase services operated by Google are used to provide authentication and associated infrastructure. The services and roles in use must be confirmed in our vendor register: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check and any later Cloud Functions or Messaging. We may share information with service providers acting under appropriate contracts and with authorities when legally required. Circle participants will only see information the implemented product specifically authorises; no sharing should be described as active before verified access-control testing.

Location permissions are not currently requested in the validated development build. If future features request location, the App will explain what is shared, with whom, for how long, and how to stop. Background location, approximate/precise location and retention rules will be disclosed before enabling them. 6. Storage, security and international transfers

Our development Firestore database was reported as configured for the Belgium (europe-west1) region. This does not establish that Firebase Authentication, security telemetry or every provider process is restricted to the EEA. International transfer arrangements and other processing locations must be confirmed against the deployed services before publication. We use access controls, encrypted transport, Firebase authentication and App Check safeguards; no internet service can promise absolute security. We will not intentionally expose secrets in the public website or source repository.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain account information while an account is active as necessary for the service, subject to applicable obligations. When deletion is implemented and operational, we aim to delete or de-identify associated profile and device data, subject to narrowly justified security and legal retention and provider backup schedules. The development backend design includes short-lived deletion markers and device-registration expiries, but these are not evidence that a deployed deletion service is operating. Specific retention schedules for account records, Circle records, diagnostic logs, backups and any future location data must be confirmed against the deployed service before public release. Development settings include seven-day device-registration leases and a 24-hour deletion tombstone, but these do not by themselves establish final data-retention practice.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your circumstances under GDPR, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to particular processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact LensLabsAI@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity. You may also complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) or another competent supervisory authority.

9. Children and age eligibility

The current MVP is designed for adults aged 18 or over. People under 18 should not register or use the service under this proposed first-release policy. The application and deployed backend must enforce the same eligibility rule before launch. Parent-managed child tracking and under-18 accounts are not included in the current scope.

We will update this page when data practices materially change, with an effective date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice or renewed consent. Questions and rights requests: LensLabsAI@gmail.com.
